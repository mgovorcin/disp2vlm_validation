The gate (PRD 2.7, decisions 0001, 0002) FAILS a frame if:
- the double-difference (DD) sill is not below raw;
- the sill rises significantly by more than 5% against gamma;
- velocity RMSE or |median bias| against MIDAS rises by more than
  0.2 mm/yr.

The velocity rules use the stations outside deforming areas and event
footprints (Venti DB `1.0-draft`). Per-frame numbers are in
[Baselines](../baselines.md#the-benchmark-gate-on-10-frames-venti-t46-2026-10-12).

## 4. Findings the reviewers should know

### 4.1 Houston (PASS with the edge-pass frame settings)
- **Gated velocity RMSE:** 5.90 against gamma's 5.51 mm/yr. Two areas drive it:
  - Galveston Island (GAL2, GAL7, TXGA, TDAM): −8 to −18 mm/yr;
  - Matagorda (TXBC, TXMG): +8.5 mm/yr.
- **Galveston is a disconnected water-mask region.** About 24% of the raw
  pairs carry a whole-cycle jump against the mainland, and the 50 km
  surface absorbs part of it.
- **The category condition is evidence-based (owner, 2026-10-12): the
  subsiding-area RMSE must drop from raw** (raw with its offset removed).
  It does not: 2.42 against 1.74 mm/yr.
- **What gamma does instead:** it carries a frame-wide +4 mm/yr bias, but
  no added scatter.
- **Unwrap correction on** brings the gated RMSE to 5.19, better than
  gamma, but it is incomplete.
- **Earlier proposal (superseded 2026-10-14):** a waiver for Houston as a
  known limitation of coastal disconnected regions. The edge pass below
  makes it pass.

**Edge pass (2026-10-13).** All runs are v0.5 + remove-restore, gated under 0002.

| F08882 | regions only | + stable-pixel mask (stack-mean coherence ≥ 0.7) | + per-pair coherence ≥ 0.7 |
|---|---|---|---|
| DD sill | 110 | **101** | 100 |
| gated median / RMSE / NMAD (mm/yr) | +0.73 / 5.35 / 4.20 | −0.44 / **5.10** / **1.78** | +0.07 / 5.91 / 2.51 |
| defo_area median / RMSE | +0.87 / 2.42 | +0.10 / **1.67** | +0.66 / 1.96 |
| subsiding RMSE (limit 6.13) | 6.28 | 6.67 | 7.81 |
| Matagorda TXBC / TXMG | +8.6 / +8.3 | **+1.4 / +0.8** | +2.0 / +1.9 |
| Galveston GAL2 / TXGA | −9.5 / −16.4 | −18.1 / −13.5 | −23.5 / −17.1 |
| verdict | FAIL | FAIL (subsiding only) | FAIL |

- **Regions** (venti-dev #32/#33, on for F08882) keep the island cycle
  off the mainland.
- **Fitting only on stable scatterers** fixes Matagorda. The 50 km
  surface was absorbing the phase-bias signal of decorrelating crop
  fields around the GNSS towns.
- **A per-pair threshold is weaker than the stack-mean mask** (it also fixes most of Matagorda, but scatter returns on the coast and Galveston). Early
  pairs have 20–30% of the frame above 0.7 (against 9% for the stack
  mean), so crop fields that are coherent for a while still enter the fit.
- **Galveston remains the open failure.** It is the sub-region error
  (T62), and it gets worse when the island offset is estimated from fewer
  pixels.

**Final (2026-10-14): island offsets from every valid island pixel.** The
packaged mask (venti-dev #35) only removes pixels from the fit. The island
offsets still use all valid island pixels, because Galveston's offset went
wrong when it came from its few stable pixels.

| F08882 | packaged mask, island offsets from stable pixels | **packaged mask, island offsets from all pixels** |
|---|---|---|
| verdict | FAIL | **PASS** |
| DD sill (vs gamma) | 96 | 102 (−60%) |
| gated median / RMSE / NMAD | +0.15 / 5.96 / 2.01 | +0.21 / **4.86** / 2.95 (gamma +4.22 / 5.51 / 1.87) |
| subsiding median / RMSE (limit 6.13) | −0.04 / 7.90 | +0.13 / **5.93** |
| Galveston GAL2 / GAL7 | −24.0 / −24.2 | −10.0 / −10.1 |
| Matagorda TXBC / TXMG | +1.6 / +1.4 | +6.5 / +6.3 |

- **Houston passes.**
- **Matagorda's stations sit in a land region of their own.** That region
  also gets an offset, which picks the crop bias up again. Matagorda is a
  documented residual.
- **Galveston's sub-region error (TXGA −17) is unchanged** (T62).

### 4.2 Central Valley
- **Without remove-restore**, the 50 km surface removes up to about
  60 mm/yr of real subsidence inside the bowl. Station RMSE rises from 17
  to 24 mm/yr.
- **With remove-restore**, the in-bowl median is +8.9 against +32.6 mm/yr,
  and the stations outside are unaffected (+2.42 / 5.04).
- The +8.9 mm/yr left inside the bowl, and the frame's +2.4 mm/yr median
  outside it, are open items.

**Edge pass (2026-10-13): San Joaquin polygon widened by 15 km**
(venti-dev #34, area DB 1.1-draft). The 10 km polygon left the bowl rim
in the fit, and the 50 km surface absorbed the rim subsidence.

| F36542, v0.5 + RR | 10 km | +15 km |
|---|---|---|
| verdict / DD sill | PASS / 103 | PASS / 107 |
| defo_area n / median / RMSE (mm/yr) | 20 / +8.86 / 34.82 | 40 / +1.42 / 20.55 |
| stable median / RMSE | +2.14 / 4.06 | +1.83 / 4.21 |

Most of the bowl residual is gone. The Kern polygons are outside this
frame and keep 10 km.

### 4.3 Ridgecrest
- **The event DB** (Mw 7.1 and 6.4 footprints) excludes spanning pairs
  from the fit.
- **With remove-restore** the gate passes (median +0.45 against gamma's
  +0.27, within the 0.2 rise limit). Plain v0.5 misses it by 0.03.

**Step leakage (PRD category 6, measured 2026-10-12).** The test regresses
CAL on DISP inside the event footprint, on 300 m blocks. In a pair spanning
the events, DISP there is dominated by the coseismic step (std 149 mm).

| pair | v0.5: slope / CAL std | v0.5 + RR: slope / CAL std |
|---|---|---|
| 2019-05-29 → 07-10 (spans both events) | **+0.44** / 77 mm | **+0.10** / 27 mm |
| 2019-05-29 → 07-04 (before the events, control) | +0.47 / 7.2 mm | +0.47 / 7.2 mm (identical) |

- **Without remove-restore, about 44% of the step leaks into CAL.** With
  it, about 10% does.
- **Where the rest comes from:** far-field coseismic motion outside the
  drafted footprint (rupture length × 0.75 buffer). Widening the footprint
  is a T41.4 / edge-pass item.
- **Pairs that don't span the event are bit-identical** with and without
  the DB (PRD 2.8).
- **The control's slope** (and ~0.9 outside the footprint) is the
  calibration removing long-wavelength DISP error, not leakage.

**Edge pass (2026-10-13): wider footprints rejected.** Buffering the
footprints to 1.5 × rupture length removes the residual leakage inside
them, but it takes GNSS constraint away from the surrounding area:

| spanning pair | current footprint | 1.5 L footprint |
|---|---|---|
| inside: slope / CAL std | +0.10 / 27.1 mm | −0.03 / 19.6 mm |
| outside: slope / CAL std | +0.85 / 17.7 mm | +0.74 / 18.9 mm |
| frame gate | PASS | **FAIL** (median +0.30 higher) |

The current footprints stay. The ~10% leakage is accepted and documented.

### 4.4 VLM (product built on v0.5 DISP-CAL)
- **LA (F16940 + F18904):** vertical rate against GNSS Up, median −0.42,
  NMAD 1.44, RMSE 2.67 mm/yr on 172 stations seen by both looks; 80% are
  within 2 mm/yr. It meets the owner's 1–2 mm/yr target.
- **New York:** the descending DISP has a gap from 2020 to 2022, so most
  pixels come from a single look. That still gives a rate NMAD of 1.3 mm/yr.

### 4.5 Uncertainty
- **σ_CAL** follows R-E1, calibrated by TS-G1.
- **σ_CAL is the surface-only term.** At the pair level it is tiny (median
  0.2–0.7 mm) and covers about 5% of the residuals. That is by design and
  must stay explicit in the product text.
- **The user budget is σ_CAL + 10 mm (DISP) + σ_GNSS** (TS-E4). It is
  realistic on 9 of 10 frames and slightly conservative.
- **Puerto Rico** (tropical island, troposphere uncorrected) needs about
  16 mm.
